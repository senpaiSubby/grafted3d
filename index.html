04 Questions

Before you send a brief

The short answers. Anything else, ask in the form below.

What does a project cost?

It depends on the size, the material, and how much design work the part needs. Send the details and you get a price with the design, before anything is printed.

How long does it take?

You get a timeline with the quote, before anything is printed. Complexity is usually what sets the pace, not the size of the part.

Do I need a 3D file to start?

No. A sketch, a photo, or a written description is enough to start. If you already have an STL, OBJ, 3MF, or STEP file, send it and the work starts from there.

Can you copy or replace an existing part?

For a replacement or a copy of something you already own, photos with a few key measurements are the usual starting point. We confirm dimensions before printing.

What can be printed?

Custom parts, prototypes, brackets, housings, replacements, and small batches. If it can be modeled and printed, ask — that's usually the right question to send.

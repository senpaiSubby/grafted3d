01 What we do

Design, modeling, and printing

Three parts of the same job, done in one place.

A/01 — DESIGN

3D design

Send a sketch, a photo, or a rough idea. We work out the shape, the proportions, and what the part needs to do.

A/02 — MODEL

3D modeling

We build the model, check the dimensions, and prepare the file so it prints cleanly.

A/03 — PRINT

3D printing

We print it on Bambu Lab printers, clean up the part, and hand it over.

02 How it works

From your file to a finished part

  1. 1 STEP 01

    Send it over

    A sketch, a file, or a description. Whatever you have to start with.

  2. 2 STEP 02

    We design and model

    We come back with a design and any questions before anything is printed.

  3. 3 STEP 03

    We print and hand over

    The finished part, printed, checked, and passed to you.
